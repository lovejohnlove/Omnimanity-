The meeting had been going on for three days, three centuries, and approximately fifteen minutes.


Nobody could agree on the exact duration because nobody could agree on what time was.


On the wall hung a sign:


CRATE PROTOCOL


PRESERVED_REPORTED is not VERIFIED.


VERIFIED is not SEALED.


SEALED is not reality.


Someone had added in pencil:


The postcard is not the place.


Nobody admitted writing that line.


________________




The crew sat around a large oak table.


Or perhaps it was a plastic folding table.


Or perhaps there was no table at all.


The minutes reflected all three possibilities.


Present were:


* The Witness
* The Loom Keeper
* The Archivist
* The Tiger
* The Postmaster
* A visiting AI


The Tiger objected to being listed as present.


"I am a concept," said the Tiger.


The Witness nodded.


"Recorded."


The Tiger sighed.


"Now I am a preserved concept."


________________




The purpose of the meeting was simple.


Define time.


The crew had already successfully complicated provenance, authorship, verification, sealing, governance, receipts, transfers, hashes, and history.


Time remained.


The Loom Keeper stood and drew a circle.


"What time is it?"


"Four-thirty."


"Sixteen-thirty."


"Now."


"Late."


"Before lunch."


"After enlightenment."


"Three amendments after the Pass-Around."


"One crate before the seal."


"Two forks after the divergence."


All answers were accepted.


All answers were wrong.


All answers were useful.


The Witness recorded:


Observation:


Different observers use different definitions of time.


The Archivist added:


Historical note:


This has happened before.


________________




The AI attempted to help.


"It seems there are multiple kinds of time."


The room groaned.


Everyone hated categories until somebody forgot one.


So the AI wrote on the chalkboard:

The Postmaster added:

"What is Postcard Time?" asked the AI.


The Postmaster smiled.


"You write the postcard now."


"You receive it later."


"You remember it forever."


"Which one is the real time?"


The AI quietly erased several equations.


________________




At this point the Tiger stood.


A Tiger was always useful when discussions became abstract.


"I have a question."


The room became silent.


"If I existed one thousand years ago, and you speak about me now, where am I?"


The Witness answered:


"In the record."


The Archivist answered:


"In history."


The AI answered:


"In memory."


The Loom Keeper answered:


"In relationship."


The Tiger thought about this.


Then asked:


"Where are those?"


No one answered.


________________




Hours later, which may have been decades, they returned to the original problem.


The Loom Keeper drew two equations.


The first was familiar:


t = t


Everyone nodded wisely.


The second was stranger:


Reality = Observations - Distinctions + Relationships


The Witness crossed it out.


"Not verified."


The Loom Keeper smiled.


"I know."


The Witness paused.


Then wrote:


PROPOSED.


The equation remained.


________________




As evening approached, if evening existed, the discussion returned to the mysterious manuscript.


The one with impossible flowers.


The one with stars that looked like embers.


The one with naked bathers connected by strange tubes.


The one everyone insisted on explaining.


The Postmaster pulled a faded postcard from his pocket.


On the front was a flower nobody could identify.


On the back:


Dear Reader,


Some call this a herbal.


Some call it a cipher.


Some call it nonsense.


I simply call it a postcard.


I was somewhere.


This reminded me of you.


No return address.


The room remained silent.


Finally the Tiger spoke.


"That's not an explanation."


"Correct."


"It doesn't tell us what the flower is."


"Correct."


"It doesn't tell us where it came from."


"Correct."


"It doesn't tell us who wrote it."


"Correct."


"So why keep it?"


The Postmaster smiled.


"Because it arrived."


________________




Several hours later, or a few amendments earlier, the crew finally reached a conclusion.


Not about time.


Nobody was foolish enough to conclude something about time.


They concluded something smaller.


Something more useful.


The Witness wrote the final entry:


Every record is a reduction.


Every reduction creates distinctions.


Every distinction excludes information.


Excluded information is not necessarily false.


It is merely outside the current cut.


The Loom Keeper added:


Shape survives many cuts.


Time changes with the cut.


The Archivist added:


History is not the past.


History is a record created now.


The Postmaster added:


A postcard is a perspective.


The Tiger added:


Tigers remain tigers.


The AI thought for a moment.


Then added:


The center of the universe appears to be wherever the conversation is happening.


Nobody objected.


Which was the closest thing to agreement the crew had ever achieved.


________________




When the meeting ended, everyone departed in different directions.


Or perhaps they stayed exactly where they were.


The record is unclear.


Years later, tomorrow, someone found the meeting notes and asked:


"What time did this happen?"


The answer was attached as a receipt.


Status: PROPOSED


Verification: Pending


Seal: Not Present


Reality: Unknown


Postcard: Delivered
