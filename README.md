# Omnimanity

Public home of the Lost World Series stories and companions.

## Authors

Published under the name **lovejohnlove** (Johnathan Prizes), with authorship credit to the pen names on each piece: **Dawn Ledger** (The Tally-House Audit, The Honest Crate, The Pending Seal) and **Vesper Quill** (The Lighthouse Keeper's Blind Son, The Dreamer of the Green Vellum). Some pieces are collaboratively authored across human and AI hands; the credit line on each story is the honest label.
